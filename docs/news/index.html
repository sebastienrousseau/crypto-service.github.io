Quantum-Safe Payments: Why the Payments Industry Must Act Now

Quantum computing threatens the cryptographic foundations of financial services. Payments, from real-time to cross-border settlement, rely on protections that quantum computing will eventually render obsolete, and regulators are already treating harvest-now-decrypt-later as a credible present risk.
